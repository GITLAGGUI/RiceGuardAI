About the pilot

RiceGuardAI supports rice-field monitoring through uploaded images, automated predictions, field reports, and optional alerts. It is a research and pilot system. Contact jlaggui47@gmail.com with questions about access or a report.

Using the system

Upload only material you are entitled to use and share for the selected feature. Supply accurate field information where available, respect other people’s privacy, and avoid uploading unnecessary personal information. Staff accounts and administrative tools should be used only by authorized project participants.

Predictions and field decisions

Image predictions and generated advice can be incomplete or incorrect. Results describe the submitted evidence and do not establish conditions across an entire farm. Field inspection and appropriate agricultural expertise remain necessary when deciding treatment, fertilizer use, or other crop-management actions.

Reports and alerts

Reports may be published and SMS alerts may be generated through the configured workflow. Check the report’s location, evidence and status. SMS delivery depends on recipient consent, network availability, gateway status and the mobile service. The system does not guarantee uninterrupted availability, prediction accuracy, or message delivery.

Data and access

The Privacy Notice explains the information handled by the pilot and its service providers. Google access can be revoked through Google Account settings. Account or record questions, corrections and deletion requests can be sent to the project contact.

Changes

Features and these terms can change as the research pilot develops. The date on this page identifies the current published version.
