Privacy Notice

Updated 31 October 2026

What this notice covers

RiceGuardAI is a rice-field monitoring research and pilot system. This notice describes information handled by its website, upload and disease-prediction workflow, public field bulletins, and optional SMS alerts. Contact the project team at jlaggui47@gmail.com.

Information handled

Depending on the feature you use, RiceGuardAI receives original photographs or video, file metadata and checksums, embedded GPS coordinates, manually supplied field locations, survey details, account information, model predictions, specialist review decisions, and advisory content. SMS registration also involves a mobile number, registration location, verification status, and consent or opt-out records. Avoid uploading unnecessary personal or confidential information.

Purpose and service providers

The system uses this information to store originals, process field images, display and review predictions, prepare advisories, operate the website, and deliver alerts requested by consenting recipients. The configured workflow uses Google Drive for private originals, Supabase for authentication and application records, Kaggle for image processing, and Vercel for website hosting. Evidence can also be sent to the configured advisory service, including Gemini or Ollama when that path is enabled. SMS Gate is used when SMS delivery is enabled. These services process information needed for the selected feature under their own service terms.

Google Drive access

The upload workflow requests the Google drive.file permission. This permission covers specific Drive files used with the app rather than unrestricted access to the entire Drive. Google access credentials are handled by the backend; the website does not ask for your Google password. Google Drive information is used to support the upload, storage, processing, and reporting features described here, not advertising.

Public reports and private originals

Field bulletins can include processed images, prediction summaries, advisories, and field locations shown in the report. Check the displayed location and report content before contributing information intended for publication. The original-upload folder is private; publication of a processed report is a separate system operation. Do not include people or identifying details in field photographs unless you have permission for the intended use.

Retention and requests

The current pilot does not provide a fixed automatic deletion period for all survey data. Stored records can remain while needed for the project. To ask about a record, request correction, or request deletion, contact jlaggui47@gmail.com and identify the relevant survey. Revoking Google access stops future authorized Drive operations but does not automatically delete files or records already stored by the system or its providers.

Your choices

You can revoke the Google connection in your Google Account connected-app settings. SMS recipients can withdraw consent through the website SMS preferences. Removing a connection or withdrawing alerts does not itself erase historical survey or delivery records. Uploading through the website does not itself launch model training; any additional research or training use must be covered by appropriate permission.

Updates

The project team may update this notice as the pilot changes. The date shown on this page identifies the latest published version.
